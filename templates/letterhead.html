How to Exchange Letterheads With Us

We warmly invite Rotaract and Rotary clubs worldwide to exchange letterheads with the Rotaract Club of SIMS (ASTHRA).

1
Send Your Club's Letterhead

Use our Club Connect form or email your club's official letterhead with a greeting message.

2

3
Receive Reciprocal Letterhead

Our International Service Director and President will promptly send back the official ASTHRA letterhead.
