OUR MISSION

Ignite ideas. Inspire people. Impact communities.

The Rotaract Club of SIMS (Soundarya Institute of Management & Science) is a dynamic student-led organization functioning under the global umbrella of Rotary International District 3192 and sponsored by Rotary Bangalore Chimney Hills.

We aim to create platforms where students translate empathy into action, develop executive leadership capabilities, foster lifelong international friendships, and address pressing community needs.
